## Exact final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original realtime fantasy melee VFX texture, a single UNWRAPPED horizontal sword slash ribbon on genuine transparent alpha background. Landscape 3:1 composition with generous transparent margins, no perspective, no arc/crescent: it will be mapped onto a curved 3D mesh. One continuous sharply sculpted ivory cutting edge along the top of the ribbon, left tail thin and broken, right head broad powerful pointed spear/wedge. Below edge a dark petrol steel-blue body made of 5-7 bold asymmetrical hand-painted angular brush facets, long negative-space tears exposing transparency, trailing blades peeled backwards leftwards. Narrow subdued amber secondary highlights, concentrated warm white at the leading right tip. Body tapers irregularly toward lower edge. Readable silhouette at thumbnail size, chunky painterly facets suitable for a voxel fantasy action game, excellent material craft and deliberate value hierarchy, no rainbow/neon, no smoke/fog/bloom baked outside silhouette. No scene, weapons, characters, letters, frames, grids, checkerboard, watermark, detached spark confetti. Straight horizontal strip occupying central 70 percent width and 50 percent height, fully transparent outside its strokes.
