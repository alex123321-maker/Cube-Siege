# Mine VFX art kit

`ember_burst.tres` is the reference profile for the existing remote mine. The gameplay script retains immediate detonation, 250 damage and radius 4.5. The blast radius scales presentation but is not an art-profile setting.

The presentation root owns its delta clock and cleanup. Layers: 0.11 s painted flash; four expanding flame lobes; five delayed smoke lobes; six low dust lobes; one low volumetric blast lasting 0.42 s; one MultiMesh of embers; one short local light. The blast fills the supplied radius immediately, holds for 0.09 s and cools in place (outer heat ends at 0.28 s). The volume uses 24 bounded ray samples, world-space procedural density and reconstructed opaque scene depth. It is hidden after its impulse lifetime. The central fire and smoke retain their original painted billboards and depth testing. This is a stylized density field, not fluid simulation. There is no distortion, camera shake or hit stop.

The legacy `mine_ring.gdshader`, `ring_texture` and `ring_opacity` names are retained for profile compatibility. They now control volume detail and density. The reference reuses the central fire artwork here: two filtered oblique projections gently vary heat color, while a warped 3D density field supplies curved folds and darker pockets. The volume retains 24 ray samples; reference opacity is 0.76 and the emission multiplier is 0.35. Existing Luna trials are preserved historical candidates; their ring values have not been retuned for the volume. See the [radius comparison](../../../docs/verification/vfx_mine_pilot/RADIUS_REPORT.md) and latest [visual polish](../../../docs/verification/vfx_mine_pilot/POLISH_REPORT.md).

After the impulse, a warm, broken ground imprint loses its heat by 0.58 s, becomes soot and fades by 1.45 s. Emission decays quadratically with heat so the tail reads as cooling damage rather than a lingering field of fire. `ground_lifetime` and `ground_opacity` control its duration and strength. At detonation the effect borrows meshes from intersecting `terrain` receivers, preserving the game's voxel step tops and vertical faces. A procedural material clips the imprint by 3D distance to the supplied blast origin/radius; it does not bridge height discontinuities or draw on enemies. Unloading a receiver removes its overlay, and the effect root owns all remaining cleanup. This adds one renderer per intersecting terrain mesh; repeated simultaneous blasts have not been profiled. See [ground/steps verification](../../../docs/verification/vfx_mine_pilot/GROUND_REPORT.md).

## Bounded small-model task

Create a sibling `.tres` based on the reference. Edit only exported art parameters in `scripts/vfx/mine_vfx_profile.gd`, within its ranges. Keep the script and all four texture references. Do not edit gameplay, render scripts, shaders, source art or the default profile. Preserve the warm industrial palette. Give the variant a specific perceptual goal and list exact changes. A valid file does not prove visual quality: render with the real camera in day, night and a crowd, inspect early flash/fire/smoke/tail, verify cleanup and unchanged damage. Record any parent repairs separately.

Useful tradeoffs: more emission washes out painted folds; longer opaque smoke hides enemies; a larger fire mass can compete with the damage radius; too much ring opacity makes a ground indicator instead of an explosion. Counts and phase order are fixed by the reusable effect code. Each profile has a bounded ember count (at most 40).

## Reproduce

Godot 4.6.1 Forward+, 1280x720, 60 simulation FPS. `tools/capture_mine_pilot.gd` plants and detonates the actual engineer ability five times: detail/day, game/day, game/night, crowd/night, crowd/day. Sentinels straddle the horizontal edge at radius ±0.15; all targets stand on actual terrain, and expected damage uses their true 3D positions. The crowd has targets inside and outside. Every detonation checks damage in the same call, and the final active-effect count must be zero. Optional user arguments: `--label=name --profile=res://assets/vfx/mine/trials/name.tres --radius=4.5 --only-phase=1 --no-imprint`. Radius and imprint overrides are capture-only. Use an isolated `CUBE_SIEGE_TEST_PROFILE`. See `docs/verification/vfx_mine_pilot/REPORT.md` for historical evidence, `RADIUS_REPORT.md` for the blast and `GROUND_REPORT.md` for the fading footprint on voxel steps.

## Generated art provenance

Both RGBA source textures were generated with the native Codex image-generation tool on 2026-09-29, with transparent background requested. They were copied unchanged, not painted procedurally or retouched in Python. Tint, erosion, rotation, movement and fading happen in the Godot shader/runtime. Flash reuses the generated sword contact artwork; see `../sword/README.md` for its provenance. The former sword-ribbon ring has been replaced by the density volume.
